# Translation glossary

Fixed terminology for the site copy. Spanish is the source (AGENTS.md §4, §11.5).
One table, one column per locale: add the `en` and `ca` columns here when those reviews land.
Official Catalan place names (barrios, districts) are never translated.

## Italian register

- `tu`, warm and direct, matching the Spanish tuteo. No `Lei`. Imperatives in the second person singular ("Scopri", "Raccontaci").
- Neutral wording where gender would show ("Come preferisci che ti contattiamo?", not "essere contattato").
- Say "consulenza" for the service and "consulente" for the person; "agenzia immobiliare" only where the Spanish says "inmobiliaria".
- Sentence case, no full stop on headings, `*asterisk*` emphasis on the equivalent part of the heading.
